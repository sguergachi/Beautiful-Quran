Agreement and independent status

By using Beautiful Quran, you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy. Beautiful Quran is an independent, open-source Quran reader; it is not an official application of Quran Foundation (QF), Quran.com, or QuranReflect.

Respectful use

Use the app lawfully and respectfully. Do not use, present, or combine Quranic content with material that promotes hate, extremism, misinformation, unlawful activity, sexual content, or other inappropriate material. Do not present snippets in a way that changes or misrepresents their context or meaning.

Quran Foundation content

Beautiful Quran does not currently access QF’s authenticated APIs. If that integration is enabled, QF content will be displayed only as an integral part of the reader. It will not be sold, sublicensed, offered as raw data, or used to create advertising profiles, biometric identifiers, or machine-learning models.

QF-provided Quran text will not be modified. Supported QF offline content will be kept in a local cache that checks for and applies available updates at least every seven days. The QF Developer Terms will additionally govern our use of QF APIs and QF content.

The committed reader database contains no Quran.com-derived word gloss, transliteration, QCF, or page-layout data. Current clients retrieve those fields from a legacy, unauthenticated Quran.com endpoint into a seven-day local cache. The database does contain repeat-aware timing topology derived from legacy QDC responses, normalized and verified offline and updated only through app releases. Beautiful Quran does not represent this as an approved authenticated QF integration. We are seeking QF’s written permission and will disable, migrate, correct, or remove that source if required.

Availability and corrections

Content sources, recitations, and app features may change or become unavailable. When an approved source corrects or withdraws content, Beautiful Quran may update or remove the relevant cached content. We do not promise that the app, its audio sources, or its content will always be available or error-free.

Beautiful Quran is a reading and listening aid. It does not replace qualified scholarly, religious, legal, medical, or other professional advice. Seek a qualified person for guidance specific to your circumstances.
