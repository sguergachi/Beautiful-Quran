# Tarjī detection methods

**Compare three independently selectable experiments: causal cycle tracking, weighted sinusoidal evidence, and whole-recording seeded segmentation.** Cycles can acquire without filling a fixed window; Spectrum tests periodic movement against drift; Recording uses future evidence to confirm earlier cycles. The baseline stays the default, with its decisions, guards, and audible clock preserved. Actual **Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`) completed one review**, and its required changes are incorporated below; [the full review](<Tarjī detection Opus review.md>) records the critique. The three modes are implemented and JVM/debug checks pass; release, device verification and phone delivery remain in progress. Published evidence concerns singing vibrato and signal estimation; these methods and defaults remain engineering hypotheses about acoustic held-voice modulation, not an Islamic classification or established all-reciter accuracy.

## Shared features preserve baseline behavior and live phase

Expose a reusable, allocation-free feature frame at the end of the existing hop extraction: exact hop/time, RMS20/RMS80, climax level, hold identity/age/pitch, voicing, fresh folded F0, YIN dip/threshold status, validity, and pitch-support lead. **Do not run another YIN or change the baseline's carried pitch and clarity fallback.** Fresh FM validity requires a threshold-hit estimate, voiced positive hold, finite 70–350 Hz F0, and a 40 ms pitch frame wholly inside that hold. Missing FM receives zero weight, never interpolation or forward-fill. pYIN supports retaining uncertainty before sequence tracking; its candidate/HMM machinery remains outside this compact experiment. ([Original pYIN paper](https://webspace.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/s.e.dixon/pub/2014/MauchDixon-PYIN-ICASSP2014.pdf))

All experiments use **the same evidence features**: `a[k]=ln(max(1e−5, (RMS[k−1]+2RMS[k]+RMS[k+1])/4))`, and `p[k]=1200 log₂(F0[k]/ref)`, where `ref` is the hold's first valid F0. FM receives a valid-only 1-2-1 blur with renormalized neighbor weights; invalid centres stay invalid, with zero evidence weight. This smooths measured valid features without imputing pitch. The blur adds one hop; use drifting hold pitch only for octave folding. Opus rejected RMS80 evidence because its boxcar attenuates fast modulation more strongly. Identical sensitivity knobs do not mean identical physical sensitivity to the baseline; label that difference without inverse filtering. AM measured half-excursion floor is `hA=atanh(minTremoloDepth)`; FM starts at 10 cents. Preserve **1.5–10 Hz**, volume/hold settings, and actual hop duration: 176 samples at 8,820 Hz is about 19.9547 ms.

Add one compact **experimental-only `TarjiEventStage`**; keep the baseline lifecycle intact. Its periodic open/keep evidence obeys the established hold minimum, volume off-ratio 0.7, AM-only level-balance floor 0.35, and AM-preferred locked channel with fallback only after keep loss. Preserve ten-hop no-keep persistence, extended to two periods during the first 50 event hops; ten steady hops permit another event. Preserve detection-onward climax tracking, the below-0.52-for-24-hops tail rule, same-cadence renormalization at rate ratios ≤3, existing attack/release, gain `ramp(eventHops/50)·clamp((level−0.52)/0.23)`, and 50 ms end-of-hold drying. Every open already has periodic evidence; this copy needs no unconfirmed-event machinery. `TarjiWordGate` remains authoritative.

Modes replace only decision fields: gain, event start, rate, channel, and reverberation state. **Every mode paints `TarjiEarPulse` from the live tap's identical RMS/F0 history**, never decoded-file phase or a synthesized sinusoid. Keep corrected Media3 source/sink timing, route trims, speed, smoothing/display lead, and pitch-support correction unchanged. The existing 6–16-hop pulse mean clamp remains a slow-rate limitation. ([Clock/pulse implementation](../app/src/main/java/com/beautifulquran/playback/TarjiSyncClock.kt), [detector contract](../docs/TARJI.md))

## Cycles uses separate acquisition and retention timeouts

**Proposal 1: causal, detrended extrema tracking.** Published methods measure maxima/minima distances and extent; sung cycles show unequal rise/fall durations and more regular timing than depth. This supports full-period checks without symmetric half-cycles, not transferring singing thresholds to recitation. ([Rossignol et al.](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/1999/rossignol.pdf), [Horii](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/jshr.3204.829))

Use a 96-hop within-hold ring, shrinking to about 2.5 recent periods when cadence exists. Fit a weighted line, refit once using MAD-based robust residual weights, and find Schmitt extrema confirmed by a retreat of the half-excursion floor. Refine supported turning times within ±0.5 hop. A valid-only FM blur keeps invalid centres invalid; more than two consecutive invalid hops resets FM extrema. Require ≥85% valid coverage across the tested extrema span.

Four alternating extrema give `P₀=t₂−t₀`, `P₁=t₃−t₁`, `P=(P₀+P₁)/2`. Require in-band period, `|P₀−P₁|/P≤0.20`, half-period fractions 0.20–0.80, median half-excursion ≥floor, and minimum excursion ≥35% of the median. These are **two overlapping period estimates**, not two independent cycles. Opus found the original `0.75P` staleness cutoff could flap during reversal confirmation. Revised **open** requires latest extremum age ≤`0.85P`; **keep** allows ≤`1.1P` and latest excursion ≥70% of the floor. Return separate channel evidence and existing level-balance semantics to the stage.

At 2.7 Hz, four extrema span about 556 ms plus initial phase and confirmation. The notes' feature-only exploration acquired in 0.72–0.88 s; revised thresholds, real extraction, and gates still need measurement. A 1,200 ms hold setting dominates earlier acquisition. The exploration exposed fragile 1.5 Hz behavior, so test adequate 2.5-period support without relaxing the band. Cost is bounded by ≤96 slots, tiny fits/sorts, and a few extrema, roughly 10⁴–10⁵ operations/s beyond extraction. Short slow tails, periodic same-pitch syllables, echoes, and skipped fast extrema remain limitations.

The audited Cycles and Recording implementations accept at most **1% rate-estimation tolerance** around cycle-band edges, clamping only accepted estimates back into the configured band. This addresses quadratic interpolation uncertainty with five samples per 10 Hz cycle; Spectrum's fitting bands stay strict. Out-of-band rejection remains a regression requirement.

## Spectrum requires coherence across two half-windows

**Proposal 2: weighted sinusoidal fitting versus trend.** Singing research combines spectral evidence with repeated contour motion; weighted periodograms provide the fitting basis. Our local slope, validity handling, and acquisition rules are adaptations. ([Nakano et al.](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2006/nakano06_interspeech.pdf), [Zechmeister and Kürster](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.2573))

With quality × Hann weights, compare `y=a+bt` with `y=a+bt+c cos(2πft)+d sin(2πft)`. Compute `Q=(SSEtrend−SSEperiodic)/SSEtrend`, `D=√(c²+d²)`. Project signal and both sinusoidal bases away from `[1,t]`, then solve tiny weighted systems; naïve DFT normalization fails on nonorthogonal short-window bases. Reject singular fits and negligible residual energy. **Q is explained variance, not probability.** AM and FM qualify independently; a fixed phase relationship is unsupported by source-filter research. ([Original source-filter analysis](https://academica-e.unavarra.es/server/api/core/bitstreams/4a71a939-859f-4390-a8b2-ce2185370809/content))

Opus replaced the broad dense bank with clipped, within-hold window/band pairs: **32 hops/4.5–10 Hz/0.625 Hz steps**, **64/2.25–4.5/0.31**, and **96/1.5–2.25/0.21**, approximately 28 search bins plus guard checks. Each trial requires `span≥2.25/f`; evaluate every second hop. Require the in-band winner to beat same-channel diagnostic fits over `[minHz−0.5,14]`, then refine locally. Guard coverage must actually cover that interval; the narrower proposal bands alone do not. Out-of-band AM does not veto healthy FM. Finer sampling does not create extra window resolution.

At the winner, fit each half-window with the same global time origin. Provisional coherence gates are phase difference ≤π/3, depth ratio 0.5–2, and both half-fit Q≥0.3. **Open** additionally needs full Q≥0.60, measured depth ≥floor, ≥85% coverage, and rate within one bin for three evaluations, about 120 ms. **Keep** uses Q≥0.45, phase tolerance π/2, and depth ≥70% floor. Coherence addresses the notes' high-Q bump/colored-noise failures; it does not guarantee rejection of every correlated fluctuation, and legitimate chirps can fail it.

Two-and-a-quarter cycles need 0.83 s at 2.7 Hz and 1.5 s at 1.5 Hz; 64 hops cannot honestly cover the latter. Persistence, shared blur, useful fit support, and hold gates add delay. Tables require about 30 KB before any additional diagnostic coverage; scans remain bounded small-bank work without a DSP dependency. Opus's JVM ≤150 µs/hop target is a measurement goal, not a result. Changing cadence and short/noisy holds trade confidence against latency.

## Recording separates acoustic onset from reader admission

**Proposal 3: two-sided seeded cycle segmentation with conservative recording calibration.** Reuse decoded verse PCM and the same extractor; check cancellation every 64 hops. Components follow baseline hold identity, never smoothing across boundaries. Offline research supports bidirectional context while recognizing uncertain transitions. ([Desain et al.](https://www.mcg.uva.nl/mcg-2023/papers/dhat-2/dhat-2.html), [Herrera and Bonada](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/1998/HER60.PS.pdf))

First detrend with a centred mean spanning `round(1/(minHz·hopSeconds))`, shrinking symmetrically at edges. Discover extrema, then re-detrend local neighborhoods over measured periods and rediscover cycles. Strong cycles require in-band cadence, neighboring jitter ≤0.20, full depth, ≥85% FM coverage, and AM level balance ≥0.35; weak continuation starts at 70% depth, jitter ≤0.35, and ≥70% coverage. A seed needs **two consecutive strong cycles/five extrema**. Extend through contiguous weak evidence in both directions. Bridge at most `min(120 ms, one period)` only when valid support brackets a classifier hole inside the same component; never bridge unavailable pitch or acoustic boundaries.

Estimate quiet background only from below-20th-percentile energy frames with hold clarity <0.5. Require ≥25 frames and nonzero MAD; otherwise skip calibration, including digital-silence cases. Use `floorE=max(existingFloor,minVolume,N+6·MAD)`, raising floors only. MAD measures robust spread, not true SNR or event probability. Never normalize each recording's strongest fluctuation into a guaranteed event. ([NIST scale definitions](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda356.htm))

**Run accepted regions forward through `TarjiEventStage`** before publishing decisions. Keep `acousticOnsetHop` separate from admitted event start, which cannot precede `max(regionStart,holdStart+holdMinMs)` or the current switch/adoption hop. This fixes backdating into a prior word and restores live tail/channel/gain policy after look-ahead. Future context pays roughly two cycles of evidence before playback sampling once ready; download/decode adds unknown latency. Sequence cost is approximately linear; PCM is about 32–35 KB/s, decision arrays tens of KB/minute, with bounded worker/cache budgets.

MediaExtractor and ExoPlayer can differ in priming trim. **Decoded arrays need measured alignment to the live tap before output.** After about 1.5 s of playback, compare decoded/live RMS over ±6 hops; accept a unique credible correlation peak ≥0.9, apply its source offset once, and show it diagnostically. Repetitive cycles can make alignment ambiguous, so uncertain alignment stays silent. Matching item/key and known source clock are also mandatory. Pending, unaligned, failed, gapless handoff, or out-of-support states return zero. Phase always remains live-tap phase. Capture preview includes lead-in and explicitly says **capture-local**; full-recording calibration is not reproducible from an arbitrary short capture.

## Isolated live controls and independent labels decide promotion

The implemented **global session-only** choice is `InkEngine.tarjiDetectorMode`, default Current. In reader InkLab's Tarjīʿ tab, Cycles/Spectrum/Recording toggles are mutually exclusive; all off means Current. TarjiLab shares four labels. Mode is excluded from profiles/exports, and developer-off resets Current. Replay the held word after switching. Only the selected experiment runs alongside baseline extraction. Mode changes reset experimental/word light, preserving tap counters, playback, capture and ear clocks. Generation-tagged history rejects queued old decisions; new events cannot predate switching/adoption. Keyed results/graphs clear on reciter, item, knobs or mode changes. Pending means flat and labeled, with no mislabeled baseline fallback.

Actual automated coverage includes frozen baseline hashes across three WAV fixtures/all 40 Hani offsets; identical tap history/phase across modes; feature-level AM/FM at 1.5/2.7/5/8/10 Hz and both hop cadences; still/ramp/step/glide/bump negatives; invalid pitch and slow-cycle retention; Recording admission, bounds and cancellation; constant-carrier PCM; generation/backlog rejection; alignment policy and stale Lab results. Existing clock/reader regressions also pass. Wider mixed-channel/phase/noise sweeps, short-tail/echo labels and full device scenarios remain future validation, not completed corpus coverage. Measure Pixel p95 cost, allocation, cache bytes and readiness delay separately; performance budgets remain provisional.

The **26 recordings, 13 reciters, 326.2 seconds, ayat 1:7/44:59**, establish unlabeled execution coverage only. Hani captures explicitly remain `UNLABELED`. ## Conclusion

Opus's review makes the experiments comparable and protects timing/ownership, while leaving perceptual success an open empirical question. Implementation should stay small: shared measurements, three pure evidence methods, one separate lifecycle, and explicit pending/alignment states. Publish measured outcomes honestly, then retain the winning behavior and remove losing modes and their selector when independent labels justify that decision.
