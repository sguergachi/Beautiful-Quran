### Later Pixel 10 report: 5:2 boundary edits

returns revision `5413f8f82f0c674892b0213b5f7fb90249df38418515af65b75c933190c7739b`;

unchanged; they cannot validate these new boundaries. Compared with that
topology-only candidate, mean absolute start residual falls from
191.468 to 183.048 ms (XLSR) and 184.565 to 176.145 ms (MMS), while the

  remains strong through the proposed 2,609–3,072 ms gap (−23.43 dBFS RMS).
- **Retain the reviewed phantom removal.** Keep word 14's extended 24,040 ms
  end and every genuine rewind. The device's 23,340 ms end is 986 ms before
  both models' forced end, with strong waveform energy through 24,040 ms.
- **Hold the exact word-16 boundary as uncertain.** Advancing it by 750 ms
