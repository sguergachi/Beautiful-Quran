Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Scope and identity

This Privacy Policy explains Beautiful Quran’s data practices. Beautiful Quran is an independent app, not an official Quran Foundation (QF), Quran.com, or QuranReflect application. It retrieves public Quran content through QF’s authenticated Content API. Optional QF sign-in and bookmark sync are a separate feature, enabled only with approved public-client configuration. Reading without an account remains available.

QF hosts its own account service. When you connect, we use its stable account identifier and tokens to access the agreed bookmark collection; the app does not ask for your password or profile-editing permissions.

Sensitive religious information and consent

Bookmarks, notes, reading activity, and other interactions with religious content may be sensitive personal data. Guest bookmarks, notes, settings, searches and listening position stay on your device; Android may back up guest settings, bookmarks and notes when device backup is enabled. Optional QF bookmark sync requires a separate, affirmative in-app consent to sending religious bookmark activity and an affirmation that you are 18 or older, before hosted sign-in.

Connection sends future changes to the QF library; it does not upload existing device bookmarks. “Add device bookmarks to QF” is a separate explicit action and keeps the original device copy. QF stores the verse references and collection memberships, and other connected QF apps may access that collection. We do not promise that the collection is private or end-to-end encrypted. Notes, reading history, preferences and exact playback state are outside this integration.

We will never sell, mine, or repurpose QF user data, and we will not use notes, reflections, or other user-generated content to train an AI model without separate, specific consent.

Network services and sharing

The public site and web reader are hosted by GitHub Pages. Android and web request public Quran content through a narrowly restricted Cloudflare Worker, which authenticates to the Quran Foundation Content API, and stream recitation audio from EveryAyah. Standard requests expose ordinary connection data, such as IP address and User-Agent, to the service receiving the request. The Worker sends QF only the content request and application credential; it never sends reading position, bookmarks, notes, searches, or another user identifier. The site’s fonts and Quran databases are served from the same GitHub Pages origin; we do not use Google Fonts or a third-party JavaScript/WASM CDN.

Optional account requests go directly over HTTPS to QF’s OAuth and User APIs, using public Authorization Code with PKCE. QF receives your authorized account/token, verse references, collection actions and ordinary connection data; the developer and Content Worker receive none of that account data. QF’s hosted sign-in and other sites apply their own privacy/cookie practices, including processing outside your home country. Review the QF account privacy notice and account terms. We do not add analytics to the app.

App-held notes, reading history and search queries are not sent to these services. If you choose an external Quran, corpus, lexicon, dictionary, GitHub, or other reference link, that destination receives the ordinary connection data necessary to open it and applies its own privacy policy.

On Android, the operating system may back up app settings, bookmarks, and notes to the backup service selected in your device settings—commonly a Google account governed by the Google Privacy Policy—and may restore them after reinstall or device transfer. Beautiful Quran’s developer cannot access that backup. Recitation audio and the QF content cache are excluded from Android cloud backup.

Repeat-aware timing in the current bundled reader is prepared offline by the maintainer. It makes no runtime timing request and sends no reading activity. Written permission for distribution of legacy QDC-derived topology has not been obtained; a separately maintained authenticated timing transition is being verified.

QF Content API requests retrieve public content only. GitHub, Cloudflare, and the other providers named here operate under their applicable service and data-protection terms. They are required to protect data they process; Beautiful Quran does not authorize them to misuse Quranic content or app user data.

Security

Local data is protected by Android app sandboxing and the device’s security controls; any Android backup is protected under the selected backup provider’s safeguards. API traffic uses HTTPS, QF credentials are encrypted in Cloudflare’s secret store and never shipped to clients, access is least-privilege and endpoint-restricted, and exposed credentials are rotated. The application does not write request bodies, credentials, or Quran content to Worker logs.

Optional account tokens never enter the Content Worker. Android encrypts the account store using AES-GCM and an AndroidKeyStore key, outside backup. Browser tokens are kept only in memory; reload requires sign-in to resume synchronization. The browser’s offline account journal contains verse references and pending changes and remains protected by your browser/device controls, rather than a claim of secure hardware storage. A single writer tab serializes account edits and refresh. OAuth callback query data is removed and excluded from service-worker caching.

Any actual or suspected unauthorized access, security breach, or data exposure involving QF APIs will be reported to QF within 24 hours. The integration is operated in accordance with Security Rule 6.9.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Guest device data remains until you delete it, clear the app/site storage, or uninstall the app. Individual bookmarks and notes can be removed in the reader. Android guest backups follow your backup provider’s controls and may be restored unless you disable or remove the backup. We hold no developer-operated cloud copy of your account, bookmarks or notes.

Settings → Quran Foundation provides export, individual bookmark changes, “Remove synced bookmarks” and disconnect. Export contains the active verse library. Removing synced bookmarks detaches matching Hafs verse memberships in the Beautiful Quran collection, preserving other collections, history and device saves. Offline removal remains queued and is complete only after QF confirms it; clearing app/site storage first cancels the local queue, so use QF’s controls to complete remote removal in that case.

Disconnect withdraws this device/browser’s participation, immediately stops sync and clears its account cache and queued edits. Android also removes the encryption key. The app attempts public token revocation and tells you if it cannot confirm completion; remove app access in QF if that request fails. Disconnect keeps your QF collection and restores guest bookmarks. Account-wide erasure, other apps’ copies, provider retention and QF backups are managed through QF’s account controls and policy, not by deleting this app’s local copy. Browser sign-in transactions expire after ten minutes; cached account bookmarks remain until disconnect or clearing site storage.

Any developer-controlled QF personal-data copies subject to a deletion request will be hard-deleted within 30 days and backups deleted within 90 days; this integration operates no such server copies or backups. These deadlines do not describe QF’s separate retention. Runtime word/QCF content still uses its independent six-day refresh/seven-day cache limit; bundled timing changes through an application update.

Children and international transfers

Beautiful Quran is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children into a developer-operated service. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Quran Foundation, EveryAyah, a user-selected Android backup provider, and destinations opened through external links may process connection or backup data outside the user’s home country under their respective policies and transfer safeguards. Where required, those transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent lawful mechanism, and the project will comply with applicable local privacy law.

Optional shared QF accounts are restricted to adults aged 18 or older pending provider eligibility clarification. Guest reading does not require registration.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date before material data-practice changes take effect, and will notify users in the app. For privacy requests, including access or correction questions, contact sguergachi@gmail.com or open an issue in the Beautiful Quran GitHub repository. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Beautiful Quran is an independent open-source project without a public business address. We provide email and GitHub Issues as its public contact channels.
