Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Scope and identity

This Privacy Policy explains Beautiful Quran’s data practices. Beautiful Quran is an independent app, not an official Quran Foundation (QF), Quran.com, or QuranReflect application. It does not currently access QF’s authenticated APIs. This policy also states the controls that will apply before a content-only QF API integration is enabled.

Data we store and why

DataWhere it staysPurpose
Settings and reading positionYour device and, when enabled by you, its Android backup serviceRestore the reader’s appearance, reciter, and place.
Bookmarks and notesYour device and, when enabled by you, its Android backup serviceSave passages and annotations you choose to create and restore them after reinstalling or changing devices.
Quran databases and audio cacheYour deviceMake reading and recently heard recitation available offline.
Public QF content and sync checkpoint, if enabledYour deviceKeep approved content current through Content Sync. QF is not sent your bookmarks, notes, reading history, or other user data.

We do not operate accounts, collect email addresses, use analytics or crash reporting, sell personal information, or build advertising profiles. Public Quran text and related metadata obtained from QF will be retrieved as content; QF is not the source of user personal data for this app.

Sensitive religious information and consent

Bookmarks, notes, reading activity, and other interactions with religious content may be sensitive personal data. Beautiful Quran does not transmit them to its developer or QF today. Android may back up settings, bookmarks, and notes when device backup is enabled. Before an authenticated QF integration is released, the app will require separate, explicit, affirmative consent before enabling notes or processing other sensitive religious information. This consent will not be bundled with acceptance of these Terms, and its withdrawal flow will address both the device copy and Android backup behavior.

We will never sell, mine, or repurpose QF user data, and we will not use notes, reflections, or other user-generated content to train an AI model without separate, specific consent.

Network services and sharing

The public site and web reader are hosted by GitHub Pages. The current Android and web readers stream recitation audio from EveryAyah. Standard network requests expose ordinary connection data, such as IP address and User-Agent, to the service delivering the site, database, fonts, or audio. The site’s fonts and Quran databases are served from the same GitHub Pages origin; we do not use Google Fonts or a third-party JavaScript/WASM CDN.

We do not send these services app-held notes, bookmarks, reading history, or search queries. If you choose an external Quran, corpus, lexicon, dictionary, GitHub, or other reference link, that destination receives the ordinary connection data necessary to open it and applies its own privacy policy.

On Android, the operating system may back up app settings, bookmarks, and notes to the backup service selected in your device settings—commonly a Google account governed by the Google Privacy Policy—and may restore them after reinstall or device transfer. Beautiful Quran’s developer cannot access that backup. Recitation audio and the future QF content cache are excluded from Android cloud backup.

The source repository includes an optional transitional server that caches requests to a legacy, unauthenticated Quran.com content endpoint. It is not connected to the released app. Its application logs are designed to omit IP addresses, authorization headers, query strings, response content, and reading activity. Before that server receives production traffic, this policy will name its hosting provider and link the provider’s privacy policy; the provider will necessarily process ordinary connection data to deliver requests.

If enabled, QF Content API requests will retrieve public content only. Every processor that handles personal data for a future integration will be named here with a direct privacy-policy link before that integration launches. We will have an appropriate data-protection agreement with each such processor, requiring protection of user data, respect for Islamic content guidelines, and no misuse of Quranic content.

Security

Current local data is protected by Android app sandboxing and the device’s security controls; any Android backup is protected under the selected backup provider’s safeguards. Before QF API use, the integration will use TLS 1.2 or later in transit, encryption at rest, least-privilege access controls, protected secret storage, and a defined secret-rotation cadence. We will keep only redacted operational logs necessary to operate and audit the integration.

Any actual or suspected unauthorized access, security breach, or data exposure involving QF APIs will be reported to QF within 24 hours. Our future QF integration will comply with Security Rule 6.9.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Current device data remains until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. You can remove individual bookmarks and notes in the app, or remove all device data through Android’s app-storage controls. Android backup copies follow the retention and deletion controls of your selected backup provider and may be restored later unless you disable or remove that backup. Beautiful Quran has no user account, developer-operated cloud storage, or developer-held copy of this data.

Any QF offline content will use a separate local cache and sync state. It will check for and apply changes at least every seven days, and will be deleted when QF access is terminated or revoked. If we ever introduce QF user scopes or a service that stores user data, we will first add in-app access, correction, token-revocation, and “Delete My Data” controls; hard deletion will occur within 30 days and backup deletion within 90 days.

Children and international transfers

Beautiful Quran is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children into a developer-operated service. GitHub Pages, EveryAyah, a user-selected Android backup provider, and destinations opened through external links may process connection or backup data outside the user’s home country under their respective policies and transfer safeguards. Any future QF integration and its processors will use appropriate legal safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent mechanism where required, and comply with applicable privacy law.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date before material data-practice changes take effect, and will notify users in the app. For privacy requests, including access or correction questions, contact sguergachi@gmail.com or open an issue in the Beautiful Quran GitHub repository. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Beautiful Quran is an independent open-source project without a public business address. We provide email and GitHub Issues as its public contact channels. Before QF API launch, this page will be updated with the final list of any personal-data processors.
